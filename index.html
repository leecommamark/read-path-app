How Read Path works

You already speak Cantonese. Read Path teaches you to read it, using texts you choose.

  1. Find your level
    Already read some characters? Take the placement test first, so Read Path skips what you know. It takes a few minutes.
  2. Add a text
    Paste anything: a message, a menu, a news story. It stays on your phone.
  3. Calibrate
    Tap the characters you already know. Known characters carry over to every text.
  4. Learn
    Meet five new characters at a time, the most useful first, then get quizzed on them straight away. Review covers everything in the text you haven't learned yet.
  5. Reader
    Read your text with jyutping over only the characters you haven't learned. As you learn, the jyutping disappears.
  6. Due today
    A short daily review, across all your texts, so characters stick.

Your progress is stored only on this phone.

Add Read Path to your home screen — on iPhone, Share then "Add to Home Screen"; on Android, the menu. It opens like an app, works with no signal at all, and is the safest place for your progress to live.

Wherever you run it, use "Export progress" now and then. It is the only real backup.

Placement test
